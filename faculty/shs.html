Academic Track

Designed for learners pursuing university/college degrees or specialized field apprenticeships. Combines the 5 Grade 11 Core Subjects with flexible discipline elective clusters—including Arts, Design, and Sports electives—plus optional 320-hour Field Experience / Work Immersion.

Track 2 • Employment, Entrepreneurship & TESDA NC II

Technical-Professional (TechPro) Track

Formerly the TVL Track—strengthened for immediate employability, entrepreneurship, and middle-level skills certification. Pairs the 5 Grade 11 Core Subjects with intensive 320-hour TechPro electives and 320–640 hours of mandatory Grade 12 Work Immersion.
