About the School

Learn about our history, profile, principals, and organization.

History

Sta. Cruz Pingkian High School traces its roots to a modest learning community established to serve the growing educational needs of the barangay. From its humble beginnings—with only a few classrooms and a small teaching staff—the school steadily grew into a respected institution committed to quality basic education.

Sta. Cruz Pingkian High School started in the year 1968 as a Community School. It was a branch school of Nueva Vizcaya State Institute of Technology (NVSIT) before it became Pingkian High.

Over the years, SCPHS embraced innovations in teaching, strengthened community partnerships, and improved its facilities to provide a safe and nurturing environment for learners.

Today, the school stands as a beacon of hope and learning, dedicated to excellence and community service.

School Profile

Sta. Cruz Pingkian High School is a public educational institution offering programs aligned with the K–12 Basic Education Curriculum of the Department of Education.

  • Levels offered: Junior High School and Senior High School
  • Curriculum: DepEd K–12 Curriculum
  • Core values: Integrity, Discipline, Respect, Community Service
  • Support: Active stakeholder engagement and modernized learning environments

DepEd Quality Policy Statement

The Department of Education is committed to provide learners with quality basic education that is accessible, inclusive, and liberating through:

  • Proactive leadership
  • Shared governance
  • Evidence-based policies, standards, and programs
  • Responsive and relevant curricula
  • Highly competent and committed officials, teaching and non-teaching personnel
  • An enabling environment

The Department upholds the highest standards of conduct and performance to fulfill stakeholders' needs and expectations by adhering to constitutional mandates, statutory, and regulatory requirements, and sustains client satisfaction through continuous improvement of the Quality Management System.
