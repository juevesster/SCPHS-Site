Lecture Notes

📝 Generated Draft

Opening Hook (2 min): Ask students: "Have you ever wondered how a simple wire becomes the heart of a working electronic circuit?"

Core Content (8 min):

  • Define electronic components and their functions
  • Differentiate active vs passive components
  • Identify common components: resistor, capacitor, diode, transistor
  • Explain how they work together in a basic circuit

Closing (2 min): Summarize key terms. Preview next lesson: "Reading Schematic Diagrams."

🧪 Suggested Activities

Activity 1 — Component Detective (15 min)

  • Groups of 4 receive a bag of mixed components
  • Identify each by sight and write its name & function
  • Present findings to the class

Activity 2 — Build-a-Circuit Challenge (20 min)

  • Given a simple schematic, students assemble a working LED circuit
  • Measure voltage with a multimeter
