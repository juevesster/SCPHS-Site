📋 Smart Exam Checker: User Guide

Welcome! The Smart Exam Checker is designed to make your grading workflow faster, more accurate, and integrated directly with the Electronic Class Record (ECR). This guide covers everything from selecting a class to utilizing AI for essay checking.

I. Getting Started

Before grading, make sure you have the correct Class, Subject, and Assessment selected.

1 Select Context

  • Class: Choose the class from your assigned list. Selecting a class automatically loads the students.
  • Subject: Select the subject to filter the available assessments.
  • Assessment: Choose the specific quiz or exam you are grading.
  • Student: Select the student whose paper you are currently checking.

2 Choose Assessment Type

Under the "Save To" dropdown, choose where the grade will be recorded in the ECR:

  • Written Work (WW)
  • Performance Task (PT)
  • Quarterly Assessment (QA)
Averaging Mode: The system automatically averages this new score with any existing scores recorded for that category.

II. Manual & AI Grading

A. Multiple Choice (MC)

  1. Enter the correct answers in the Key to Correction boxes. (You can also click "AI Suggest" to auto-fill).
  2. Enter the student's answers in the Student Answers boxes.
  3. Click "AI Verify" to quickly see the score and detailed breakdown, or click "Grade Now" to finalize and save immediately.

B. True/False (T/F)

Similar to MC, but you will input T or F in the respective fields. The system checks for exact matches.

C. Matching Type

  1. Select the Format: Numbers to Letters (1 → A) or Letters to Numbers (A → 1).
  2. Enter the key and the student's matching answers.

D. Identification

  1. Enter the correct words in the Key boxes.
  2. Type the student's answer. Don't worry about minor typos! Click "AI Verify & Spell Check"—the system uses fuzzy matching to detect near-misses (e.g., "Photosinthesis" instead of "Photosynthesis").
  3. If the AI detects a partial match, you can click "Auto-Correct Spelling" to fix the student's answer instantly.

III. AI Essay Evaluation

The Essay tab utilizes DeepSeek AI to transcribe, analyze, and suggest scores for handwritten or typed essays.

1 Upload or Type the Essay

  • Option A (Handwritten): Click "Upload Essay Image", select the photo of the paper, and click "AI Transcribe". The AI will read the handwriting and paste the text into the box.
  • Option B (Typed): Paste the essay text directly into the text box.

2 AI Analysis

Click "AI Analyze". The system will check the essay against your Rubric and Max Score.

The AI will return:

  • Suggested Score (out of the max score).
  • Analysis (Strengths, Areas for Improvement).
  • Detected Language (Supports English, Tagalog, and Taglish).

3 Teacher Final Say

Review the AI's suggested score. You are the final judge! You can adjust the score in the Teacher Workspace box and add personalized feedback.

Click "Save to ECR" when you are satisfied.

IV. Advanced Features

Feature How to Use Benefit
Save Key to DB Click the Save Key button after entering the answer key. Saves the key to the database so you don't have to re-type it when checking the next student.
Load Key from DB Click the Load Key button. Automatically fills the answer key boxes with the saved answers.
Apply to All Click Apply to All. Quickly copy the current key to all other question types (if applicable) or sync across tabs.
Vision Upload Use the upload zone at the bottom of the MC/TF tabs. Upload a photo of the answer sheet. AI will read the shaded bubbles and fill the student's answers automatically.

V. Troubleshooting

Common Issues:
  • Student list is empty: Ensure you have selected a Class first.
  • "Not logged in" error: Your session may have expired. Refresh the page and log in again.
  • AI Transcription fails: Ensure the image is clear, well-lit, and under 5MB. Avoid shadows on the paper.
  • Grade not showing in ECR: Check the ECR Status banner at the top of the page to see if the sync was successful.
