School Memorandum

No. 01, s. 2026

To:
All Teaching and Non-Teaching Personnel
From:
The Office of the School Principal
Subject:
GUIDELINES ON SCHOOL DTR SYSTEM OPERATION
Date:
 ____ day of ________________ , 2026

1. Rationale

1.1 In line with the Department of Education's continuous effort to promote efficiency, transparency, and accountability in the daily operations of the school, and pursuant to existing Civil Service Commission (CSC) rules and DepEd policies on attendance and punctuality, this Office hereby issues the following guidelines on the operation of the School Daily Time Record (DTR) System.
1.2 This memorandum aims to:
(a) Standardize the process of recording daily attendance of all teaching and non-teaching personnel;
(b) Ensure accurate, timely, and tamper-proof recording of time-in and time-out;
(c) Strengthen compliance with official working hours as prescribed by the CSC; and
(d) Provide an orderly reference for administrative monitoring and reporting.

2. Coverage

2.1 This guideline shall apply to all teaching and non-teaching personnel of Sta. Cruz Pingkian High School, including:
(a) Permanent, temporary, and provisional employees;
(b) Contractual and job-order personnel assigned to the school; and
(c) Any personnel rendering service within the school premises for official purposes.

3. Official Working Hours

3.1 The official working hours shall be from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Mondays to Fridays, inclusive of a one (1) hour lunch break.
3.2 A flexible time scheme may be availed of in accordance with existing CSC rules and DepEd policies, subject to prior approval of the School Principal.
3.3 A grace period of fifteen (15) minutes shall be observed for the morning time-in only. Tardiness beyond the grace period shall be recorded.

4. DTR System Operation

4.1 Time-In and Time-Out Logging
(a) All personnel must personally log in their time-in upon arrival at the school and time-out upon leaving, using the designated DTR system (biometric, digital, or manual logbook, as applicable).
(b) Proxy logging (logging in on behalf of another) is strictly prohibited and shall be dealt with accordingly.
4.2 Recording of Official Travel and Field Work
(a) Personnel on official travel must file the corresponding Authority to Travel and attach it to their DTR for the covered period.
(b) Official travel hours shall be credited as hours worked, provided supporting documents are submitted within three (3) working days upon return.
4.3 Leave of Absence
(a) All leaves shall be filed using the prescribed CS Form No. 6 (Application for Leave) and approved by the School Principal in advance.
(b) Emergency leaves shall be filed immediately upon return to duty.
4.4 Undertime and Tardiness
(a) Undertime and tardiness shall be recorded and shall be subject to the corresponding deduction and/or administrative action.
(b) Personnel may offset undertime with approved compensatory time, subject to existing CSC rules.

5. Responsibilities

5.1 School Personnel — Log in accurately and promptly; report any malfunction of the system to the ICT Coordinator.
5.2 ICT Coordinator — Maintain the DTR system, ensure its proper functioning, generate reports, and safeguard data integrity.
5.3 School Administrative Officer / DTR In-Charge — Consolidate, verify, and submit monthly DTR reports to the School Principal.
5.4 School Principal — Review, approve, and sign consolidated DTR reports; monitor personnel compliance with these guidelines.

6. Reporting and Monitoring

6.1 DTR summaries shall be generated every end of the month and submitted to the School Principal for review and approval.
6.2 Summary reports shall be posted on the school's transparency board, subject to the provisions of the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).
6.3 Any personnel may request a copy of their own DTR from the DTR In-Charge for personal records.

7. Prohibited Acts

7.1 The following acts are strictly prohibited and shall be subject to disciplinary action:
(a) Falsification of DTR entries;
(b) Proxy logging (logging in/out on behalf of another);
(c) Tampering with DTR equipment, software, or records;
(d) Deliberate non-logging of time-in or time-out without justification; and
(e) Any act that violates CSC rules on attendance and punctuality.

8. Effectivity

8.1 This memorandum shall take effect immediately upon issuance and shall remain in force until otherwise revoked or amended. All prior memoranda inconsistent with this issuance are hereby superseded.

9. Compliance

9.1 All concerned personnel are directed to strictly comply with these guidelines. For questions or clarifications, please coordinate with the School Administrative Officer or the ICT Coordinator.
