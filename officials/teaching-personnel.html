Teaching Personnel

Licensed Educators • Instruction & Assessment

Overview

Teaching personnel are licensed educators responsible for delivering quality instruction, assessing learning, and ensuring effective classroom management. They play a vital role in shaping student learning and supporting the school’s academic programs.

Functions

  • Deliver standards‑aligned instruction across learning areas.
  • Assess learning outcomes and support diverse learners.
  • Collaborate on curriculum planning and school initiatives.
  • Promote positive classroom environments and student growth.
