Non-Teaching Personnel

Administrative, Support, and Operational Staff

Overview

Non-teaching personnel provide essential administrative, operational, and support services that enable the school to function efficiently and safely. They help ensure that teachers can focus on instruction and learners receive a supportive school environment.

Functions

  • Provide administrative and operational support.
  • Manage records, finance, and facilities.
  • Support ICT, library services, guidance, and school programs.
  • Help maintain a safe, efficient learning environment.
