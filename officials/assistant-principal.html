Overview

The Assistant Principal supports the Principal in leading the school's academic and operational programs, helping ensure a safe, supportive, and effective learning environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assists in supervising teachers and non-teaching staff.
  • Coordinates schedules, programs, testing, and campus events.
  • Supports student services, guidance, and discipline systems.
  • Monitors implementation of school and division policies.
