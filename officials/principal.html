Overview

The School Principal leads the school community, ensuring that teaching, learning, and operations are managed effectively and in alignment with the Department of Education's goals and standards.

Key Responsibilities

  • Leads school improvement planning and implementation.
  • Supervises faculty, staff, and academic programs.
  • Oversees curriculum delivery, student services, and school operations.
  • Ensures a safe, inclusive, and supportive learning environment.
