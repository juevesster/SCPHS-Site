How the Grading Workflow Works
▼

Follow these steps to ensure grades are calculated, reviewed, and released correctly according to DepEd DO 015.

1. Teacher (You)

Enter scores manually or click "Push to ECR" from the Assessment Hub. Once complete, click Save Scores, then click Submit for Review.

2. Adviser

The Adviser goes to the Approvals page, reviews the computed grades, and clicks Approve. (If there is an error, they click Return for Revision).

3. Student

Only after the Adviser approves will the grades become visible on the Student's Portal Dashboard and Report Card.
