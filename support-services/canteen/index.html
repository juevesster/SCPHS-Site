Canteen & Patron Guidelines

  • Form an orderly queue and observe courtesy during break and lunch periods.
  • Keep tables and surrounding grounds clean; segregate and dispose of waste properly.
  • Follow official pricing, receipt/cashless procedures, and healthy food choices.
  • Ensure food handlers wear hairnets, aprons, and clean attire with valid health/sanitation compliance.

Legal & Policy Basis for School Canteen Operations

DepEd Order No. 13, s. 2017

Healthy Food and Beverage Choices in Schools

Categorizes food and beverages sold in schools into Green, Yellow, and Red choices to promote learner nutrition, prevent obesity and diet-related illnesses, and eliminate sugar-sweetened or nutrient-poor items.

Presidential Decree No. 856

Code on Sanitation of the Philippines

Establishes sanitation standards for food establishments, proper food storage/handling, potable water, waste management, and sanitary health certificates for food handlers.

Republic Act No. 10611

Food Safety Act of 2013

Strengthens food safety protection across preparation, handling, storage, and service so learners and staff receive safe, wholesome food.

DepEd School Canteen Management Guidelines

Transparency, Affordability & Learner Welfare

Ensures affordable student budget meals, clear price posting, proper accounting of canteen funds/proceeds, and coordination with school health personnel.
