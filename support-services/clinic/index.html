Legal & Policy Basis for School Health Services

Presidential Decree No. 856 (Chapter VI)

Code on Sanitation of the Philippines

Mandates school sanitation and health services, including adequate health facilities, periodic health checks, first aid, and coordination with local health authorities.

Republic Act No. 11223

Universal Health Care (UHC) Act

Integrates population-based and individual-based health services through Primary Care Provider Networks (such as Rural Health Units / DOH facilities) with school-based health promotion and referral pathways.

DepEd Order No. 13, s. 2017 & SHNP / SF8

School Health, Nutrition & Healthy Food Policy

Guides learner nutritional assessment (Baseline, Midline, and Endline cycles), healthy food and beverage standards in schools, and standardized health reporting.

RA 10152 • RA 10627 • RA 11036

Immunization, Child Protection & Mental Health

Supports public health coordination with DOH/RHU health programs, safe/anti-bullying school environments, and campus mental health awareness and referral protocols.
