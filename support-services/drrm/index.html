DepEd Order No. 014, s. 2026 — 4H Learning Continuity in Emergencies Framework
School Learning & Service Continuity Plan (LSCP)

Pursuant to DepEd Order No. 014, s. 2026 (Guidelines on Learning Continuity in Emergencies) and Executive Order No. 130, s. 2026, the School Head determines the active continuity level (reported within 12 hours of disruption) so learner safety comes first while preventing learning loss:

LEVEL 1 • HAYO (Continue)

Full Curriculum Delivery

Safe conditions; no TCWS or suspension. Regular face-to-face classes, full lesson guides, and standard assessments continue.

Trigger: Normal / Yellow Rainfall (unless LGU/School suspends)
LEVEL 2 • HINAY (Ease-In)

Flexible & Paced Learning

Minor/moderate disruption. Narrow goals to essential competencies, reduce daily subjects/workload, send 1-week printed packets/modules home, and defer non-essential exams.

Trigger: TCWS #1–2 (grade-level suspension) or Localized Disruption
LEVEL 3 • HINGA (Check-In)

Psychosocial & Low-Stakes Check-In

High stress or ongoing recovery. Prioritize learner/staff safety & MHPSS well-being checks within 12 hours, family kits, and optional/descriptive feedback (no mandatory synchronous classes).

Trigger: TCWS #3 or Orange/Red Rainfall (In-person suspended)
LEVEL 4 • HINTO (Stop)

Total Academic Freeze

Severe danger to life and basic needs. Total suspension of in-person, online, and modular tasks. Focus strictly on rescue, relief, evacuation, and basic needs.

Trigger: TCWS #4–5, Major Quake (Intensity VI+), or Severe Calamity
