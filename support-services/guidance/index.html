📂 Guidance Services

The heart of the guidance program. It enables students to develop their abilities in personal and social adjustment, as well as academic planning and career decision-making.

Designed to gather all reliable data, information, and records, and assemble and compile these materials for their functional use.

Materials are made available to aid students in making appropriate choices, plans, and decisions.

This guidance service assists students in determining the areas of their life that need further adjustment.

Placement service will aid students in making choices consistent with their abilities, aptitudes, interests, and skills.

Helps prepare students develop their full potential through self-awareness, career planning, exploration, decision-making, and goal setting.

Gives students a realistic appraisal of their strengths and weaknesses, abilities, interests, and type of personality. It also determines the effectiveness of the implementation program.
