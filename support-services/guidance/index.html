Services

Counseling

Individual and group counseling sessions addressing personal, academic, and social concerns.

Career Guidance

Career orientation, life skills programs, and pathways planning for Junior and Senior High School learners.

Referrals & Coordination

Coordination with parents, guardians, teachers, and external professionals for learner support.
